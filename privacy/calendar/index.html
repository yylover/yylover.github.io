隐私政策

最后更新日期：2026年3月

1. 引言

欢迎使用轻简日历（以下简称"本应用"）。我们非常重视您的隐私保护。本隐私政策旨在向您说明我们如何收集、使用、存储和保护您的个人信息。使用本应用即表示您同意本隐私政策的条款。

2. 信息收集

2.1 我们收集的信息

  • 用户创建的内容：您在本应用中添加的重要日期、倒计时、日程安排等数据
  • 应用使用数据：应用崩溃日志和性能数据（仅用于改进应用体验）

2.2 我们不收集的信息

  • 个人身份信息（姓名、身份证号、手机号等）
  • 位置信息
  • 联系人信息
  • 设备唯一标识符
  • 相册、麦克风、相机等敏感数据

2.3 信息收集方式

  • 所有信息均由您主动输入或应用自动生成
  • 我们不会通过第三方渠道收集您的个人信息

3. 信息使用

我们仅将收集的信息用于以下目的：

  • 提供应用的核心功能（日历显示、倒计时、提醒等）
  • 同步您的重要日期和日程安排
  • 改善应用性能和用户体验
  • 修复应用 Bug

我们不会将您的信息用于任何其他商业目的或与第三方共享。

4. 信息存储

4.1 本地存储

  • 所有用户数据存储在您的设备本地
  • 数据使用 SwiftData 框架进行管理
  • 您的数据仅在本设备上访问和使用

4.2 云存储

  • 本应用不提供云端数据同步功能
  • 所有数据不会上传到任何服务器

4.3 数据安全

  • 数据存储在设备的安全区域内
  • 我们采用行业标准的安全措施保护您的数据
  • 但我们无法保证在设备丢失或被盗时的数据安全

5. 信息共享

5.1 我们不会与第三方共享您的个人信息

5.2 例外情况

在以下情况下，我们可能需要披露您的信息：

  • 遵守法律法规要求
  • 保护我们的合法权益
  • 配合政府部门或司法机关的要求
  • 防止欺诈或安全威胁

6. 您的权利

您对您的个人信息享有以下权利：

  • 访问权：您可以在应用中查看所有存储的数据
  • 修改权：您可以随时编辑或删除任何重要日期和日程
  • 删除权：您可以通过卸载应用删除所有数据
  • 知情权：您可以随时了解我们如何使用您的信息

7. 数据保留

  • 您的数据将一直保存在您的设备上，直到您主动删除或卸载应用
  • 卸载应用后，所有本地数据将被清除
  • 我们不会保留任何已删除的数据副本

8. 儿童隐私

  • 本应用不针对 13 岁以下的儿童
  • 我们不会故意收集儿童的个人信息
  • 如果我们无意中收集了儿童信息，我们会立即删除

9. 隐私政策更新

  • 我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策
  • 更新后的政策将在应用内发布，并注明修改日期
  • 继续使用本应用即表示您接受更新后的隐私政策

10. 联系我们

如果您对本隐私政策有任何疑问或建议，请通过以下方式联系我们：

  • 邮箱：quickyangjob@gmail.com
  • 应用名称：轻简日历

我们将在收到您的反馈后尽快回复。

English Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Calendar (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy is intended to explain how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information. By using this App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information We Collect

  • User-Generated Content: Important dates, countdowns, schedules, and other data you add to the App
  • App Usage Data: Crash logs and performance data (used solely to improve the App experience)

2.2 Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identity information (name, ID number, phone number, etc.)
  • Location information
  • Contact information
  • Device identifiers
  • Sensitive data such as photos, microphone, or camera access

2.3 How We Collect Information

  • All information is voluntarily entered by you or automatically generated by the App
  • We do not collect your personal information through third-party sources

3. How We Use Your Information

We only use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Providing core App features (calendar display, countdowns, reminders, etc.)
  • Syncing your important dates and schedules
  • Improving App performance and user experience
  • Fixing App bugs

We will not use your information for any other commercial purposes or share it with third parties.

4. Information Storage

4.1 Local Storage

  • All user data is stored locally on your device
  • Data is managed using the SwiftData framework
  • Your data is accessed and used only on your device

4.2 Cloud Storage

  • The App does not provide cloud data synchronization
  • No data is uploaded to any server

4.3 Data Security

  • Data is stored in the secure area of your device
  • We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your data
  • However, we cannot guarantee data security in the event of device loss or theft

5. Information Sharing

5.1 We Do Not Share Your Personal Information with Third Parties

5.2 Exceptions

We may need to disclose your information in the following circumstances:

  • To comply with legal requirements
  • To protect our legitimate rights and interests
  • To cooperate with government agencies or judicial authorities
  • To prevent fraud or security threats

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You can view all stored data within the App
  • Right to Modify: You can edit or delete any important dates and schedules at any time
  • Right to Delete: You can delete all data by uninstalling the App
  • Right to Know: You can learn how we use your information at any time

7. Data Retention

  • Your data will remain stored on your device until you actively delete it or uninstall the App
  • Upon uninstalling the App, all local data will be deleted
  • We do not retain any copies of deleted data

8. Children's Privacy

  • The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children
  • If we inadvertently collect information from a child, we will delete it immediately

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time
  • The updated policy will be published within the App with the modification date noted
  • Continued use of the App indicates your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

  • Email: quickyangjob@gmail.com
  • App Name: Calendar (轻简日历)

We will respond to your feedback as soon as possible after receiving it.
